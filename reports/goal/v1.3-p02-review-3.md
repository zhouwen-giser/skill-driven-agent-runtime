## Blocking finding

Two concurrent transactions can allocate Outbox identity values 10 and 11, commit 11 first, let the
consumer advance its private cursor to 11, and only then make 10 visible. The lower late-visible row
would never satisfy the high-water query. The existing late-timestamp regression did not exercise
this reversed-commit boundary.
