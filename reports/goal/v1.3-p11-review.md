## Major

The first review found two Major defects:

1. `timeoutMs` reached the Provider invocation port but did not actively abort
   the transport. Commit `c62334a` added a per-step controller, upstream
   cancellation forwarding, timeout abort and regression evidence.
2. Case adaptation rejected credentials and historical IDs but not PII.
   Commit `c62334a` added recursive PII rejection.

The second review found one remaining Major: snake_case PII names were rejected,
but camelCase names such as `personalEmail` and `userId` could bypass the
matcher. Commit `dc636e6` canonicalized field names, covered camelCase
credential/history fields and changed the nested scan to one depth-bounded
pass.

All Major findings are closed. Focused regression is 41/41 and the final
exact-commit full verification passes.

## Accepted

1. P10 precheck/retrieval/deadline/fallback authority order is unchanged.
2. Case similarity is ranking-only; failure boundaries fall back or confirm.
3. Case candidates enter formal authority only through P08.
4. Provider Registry/readiness owns secret-free Profiles.
5. Capability, classification, residency, schema and capacity are hard gates.
6. Cascade is serial, finite, budgeted, actively cancellable and discards late
   or stale results.
7. Credentials are decrypted only at the existing transport boundary and are
   absent from P11 evidence/API projections.
8. PostgreSQL owns immutable evidence and Outbox; P11 creates no second
   Planner, Policy, Artifact, Outcome, Skill or MCP authority.
