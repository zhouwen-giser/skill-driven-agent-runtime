## Review method after deployment

1. bind every observation to exact release SHA, tenant-authorized cohort and
   Artifact/Rule/Template/Case/Model version;
2. compare against a formal Outcome/control cohort, not model
   self-evaluation;
3. separate fast-selected, fast-committed and fallback cases;
4. inspect correction/recovery and dependency/policy changes;
5. record uncertainty and counterexamples;
6. recommend only continue, collect more data, request revalidation, disable
   fast path or open a next-version candidate.

## Authority boundary

P14 cannot change Artifact status or pointer, patch a Rule/Template/Case/Model
Route, or directly enqueue a promotion. Any recommendation goes through the
existing P06/P02 governance path and explicit human authorization.
