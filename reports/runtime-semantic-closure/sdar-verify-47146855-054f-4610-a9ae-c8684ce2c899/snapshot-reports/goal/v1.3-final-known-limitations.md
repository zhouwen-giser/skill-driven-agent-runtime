# SDAR v1.3 Release-Candidate Known Limitations

These limitations are part of the candidate decision and may not be interpreted
as hidden production guarantees.

## Deployment and security boundary

- The v1 baseline is a trusted-intranet deployment. A2A and the legacy
  Management surface do not become an Internet authentication perimeter in
  P13. Non-loopback binding requires an explicit risk acknowledgement.
- Artifact management uses an optional deployment-owned Bearer identity in the
  standard Server. Production secret distribution, rotation, revocation and an
  organization identity provider remain deployment responsibilities.
- No multi-tenant authentication system or public Internet hardening is added;
  tenant enforcement begins only after a trusted principal is resolved.
- Break-glass policy, approver assignment and operator lifecycle are
  organization controls outside the repository.

## Capacity and availability

- Capacity, recovery time and data-loss measurements are from an isolated local
  Windows/Docker environment with synthetic data. They are not production SLO,
  SLA, RTO or RPO commitments.
- The requirements baseline contains no approved production availability,
  latency, cost, queue-lag, RTO or RPO target. P13 reports measured values and
  leaves production thresholds unset.
- The supported active-workload baseline remains approximately one to ten
  concurrent tasks. Larger 100/1000 concurrency and 100k Artifact runs are
  engineering stress evidence, not a supported production sizing promise.
- Running in one process is intentional for V1. Running work is not
  automatically recovered or retried after process loss, except the frozen
  explicit waiting-external continuation boundary.

## Runtime behavior

- Compiled Artifacts are optimization/advisory inputs. They never replace
  confirmation, policy, capability, formal planner or LangGraph validation.
- Empty exact Artifact allowlists deny compiled retrieval. Operators must
  deliberately allow an exact approved version.
- Model provider quality, availability, price and rate limits remain external.
  The runtime preserves budget/deadline/fallback evidence but cannot guarantee
  provider service levels.
- MCP/Provider remote Task terminal truth belongs to the Provider. SDAR records
  binding/observation/control facts and does not fabricate remote terminal
  states.

## Evidence boundary

- Console evidence uses component and real HTTP vertical-slice tests; P13 makes
  no browser-pixel or screenshot claim.
- A2A evidence covers the frozen official MUST set. Skipped optional TCK cases
  are not claimed as implemented.
- PostgreSQL/Redis chaos evidence is local container restart/flush evidence, not
  a multi-node regional disaster-recovery exercise.
- Dependency and container scans describe the exact locked candidate. They do
  not predict vulnerabilities disclosed after the scan time.
- Reproducibility means two clean exact-commit builds have identical audited
  output hashes on the recorded toolchain; it is not a cross-OS reproducibility
  claim.

## Publication boundary

- P13 does not authorize merge, tag, release or deployment.
- Per the repository owner's sequencing instruction, push and the `main`
  pull-request update occur only after P14 reaches a truthful terminal state.
- Production manifest, environment, monitoring access and named release,
  operations and rollback owners are required P14 inputs; they are not inferred
  from local P13 evidence.
