## Required production inputs

- immutable deployment topology and resource limits;
- traffic, concurrency and tenant distribution;
- Provider price/quota profile and Model usage facts;
- queue, Outbox, database, Redis, storage and network metrics;
- retention window and growth history;
- incident and operator effort records;
- approved scaling and cost thresholds.

## Review policy

Attribute cost separately to Fast Path, Cognitive Fallback, Model Cascade,
shadow, replay, compiler, management and incidents. A cost recommendation must
not remove authorization, tenant, validation, confirmation, policy, recovery
or formal Outcome checks.
